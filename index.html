Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

Chocolate chip cookies are a delicious sweet treat loved by people of all ages. They are crispy on the outside and soft on the inside.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Chocolate Chips
  • Butter
  • Eggs

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, sugar and butter in a bowl.
  2. Add the eggs and chocolate chips and mix well.
  3. Shape the dough into small cookies.
  4. Bake the cookies until they turn golden brown.
